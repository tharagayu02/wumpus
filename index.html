THE ADVENTURER'S SURVIVAL GUIDE

Master the laws of Wumpus World and the art of logical deduction

💨 The Breeze & Pits

Deep, bottomless pits are scattered throughout the cavern. Falling into a pit is instantly fatal (-1000 pts). When standing in any chamber orthogonally adjacent to a pit, you will feel a BREEZE.

☣️ The Stench & The Wumpus

A monstrous, flesh-eating beast known as the Wumpus lurks in the shadows. Entering its chamber means gruesome death (-1500 pts). When adjacent to the beast, you will detect a putrid STENCH.

✨ Golden Treasure

Ancient caches of gold lie hidden in the cave. When entering the chamber containing gold, you will perceive a brilliant GLITTER. Press E or tap Grab Gold to seize it (+1000 pts)!

🏹 The Bow & Arrow

You carry a limited quiver of magical arrows. Press Space to aim and select a direction. An arrow flies in a straight line until hitting a wall or slaying the Wumpus (+500 pts)!
